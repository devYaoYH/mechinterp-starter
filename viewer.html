Class separation by layer

Distance between class means, divided by the typical activation norm at that slot. A cheap “is there anything here at all” preview — it is not a probe and controls for nothing, but a capture that stays flat everywhere will not support one.

Accuracy ceiling by position

Rows sharing an activation value must get the same prediction, so the most they can contribute is their majority label. For sampled rollouts the early positions share a prefix across samples that ended differently — that is normal, and it caps what any probe can score there. Probe where the ceiling clears the base rate.
